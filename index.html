v0.2 / local process experiment

The queue survives the interruption.

The coordinator accepts only committed fictional PNGs. Two OCR worker processes call Tesseract; a separate validation worker checks the known synthetic labels. All workers register over authenticated loopback connections on one computer.

Method and limitsInspect the synthetic report

Design ownership and proof boundary

Leonel's operating design

Leonel reports designing and operating adaptable AI workflows across machines. He decides where coordination sits, routes by capability, and preserves handoffs and recovery. He set this public demo's scope and acceptance checks; coding agents assisted with implementation.

What this repository verifies

Its synthetic tests cover deterministic IDs, legal state changes, waiting, bounded retry, duplicate protection, checksums, and delivery. Reproduce the local run and inspect the source before treating any broader private practice as independently verified.

What to examine next
